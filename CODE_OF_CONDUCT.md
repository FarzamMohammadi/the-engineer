# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive experience for everyone. All participants are expected to treat each other with respect and professionalism.

## Summary

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Show empathy toward other community members

## Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainer privately:

- **Preferred:** Open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/FarzamMohammadi/the-engineer/security/advisories/new) — only the maintainer sees the report. (The "security advisory" channel is used here because it is GitHub's standard private-disclosure path; this is not a security issue, but the channel works the same way for confidential reports.)
- **Alternative:** Email [farzamm.oss@gmail.com](mailto:farzamm.oss@gmail.com) with `[CONDUCT]` in the subject line, or contact [@FarzamMohammadi](https://github.com/FarzamMohammadi) directly through GitHub.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter.

For the full text of the Contributor Covenant v2.1, see https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
