# Session 31 — 2026-05-24

## What Happened

Slice 7 RRP sprint — single-session requirements + research + plan. Grounded the
workspace-manager and session-memory surfaces by reading every file and tracing every
consumer; discovered that the entire `KnowledgeStore` had been wired as a read-only
consumer for thirty sessions with no producer, that `getSessionChain` plus
`previous_session_id` + `resumed_from_checkpoint` columns are write-only,
and that workspace-manager carries an in-memory `Map` that silently corrupts
`baseBranch` on restart for non-default-base tasks. Produced three deliverables (slice
file, research doc, plan doc) and refined approach.md twice during the session to
codify RRP discipline ("What to hunt for", "How to present findings") and the
principle that coding-standards alignment is a planning concern, not a sweep concern.

## Decisions Made

1. **Cut the knowledge layer in full.** Pre-v1, the read-only consumer with no
   producer is dead surface. Per-task continuity is already solved by the file-based
   `thoughts/` protocol; the knowledge layer was the unbuilt cross-task half.
   Consolidate `Hybrid Semantic Memory Search` into a new
   `Cross-Task & Cross-Session Memory (supplementary)` entry in
   `docs/future-considerations.md` capturing the pivot story; retrieval (embeddings,
   graphs, hybrid scoring) stays open-ended rather than prescribed.

2. **Persist `base_branch` into `task.workspace`; drop `baseCommit` from
   `WorkspaceRecord`.** Five runtime consumers read `baseBranch` post-creation;
   `baseCommit` is read by none. The current `: ""` hack on restart silently
   corrupts every consumer if a task ever used a non-default base.

3. **Kill the in-memory `workspaces` Map; the DB is the single source of truth.**
   `WorkspaceManager` becomes effectively stateless. `registerExistingWorkspace`
   disappears — three call sites become no-ops because `getWorktreePath` and
   `getWorkspaceRecord` now read directly from `task.workspace`.

4. **Reshape `SessionMemory` as a namespace, not a forwarder.** Three stores
   (`sessions`, `journal`, `checkpoints`) as public readonly fields; drop all
   pass-through methods; drop `ISessionMemory`; rename store methods to drop the
   redundant `Journal`/`Session`/`Checkpoint` suffix. The conceptual grouping stays;
   the ceremony goes.

5. **Relocate `session-result.ts` to a new `core/session-result/` module.**
   Resolves the workspace-manager → orchestrator leaky import. Both modules consume
   the relocated utility as peers.

6. **Extract skills concerns to a new `core/skills/` module.** Skills have nothing
   to do with worktrees; the colocation was incidental. Bootstrap constructs
   `SkillsManager`; orchestrator queries it via `OrchestratorContext`.

7. **Move `removeThoughtsAndPush` to pr-manager.** PR-prep work belongs in
   pr-manager, not workspace-manager. Audit adjacent operations in pr-manager
   for natural unification during the move. Add a span when it lands (pr-manager
   already uses spans at lines 144, 248).

8. **Cut every other dead-surface item batched as one principle:**
   `getSessionChain`, `previous_session_id` and `resumed_from_checkpoint` columns,
   4 dead `JournalEntryType` values + 4 unused columns, `JournalQueryFilters`
   machinery, 2 dead `CheckpointReason` values, 2 dead `SessionEndReason` values,
   2 "for backwards compatibility" re-export blocks, `injectAuth` re-export, dead
   `push` branch in `gitExecWithAuth`. All folded into a single Decision (D8 in
   the plan).

9. **Resume flow audit, observability audit, and coding-standards application are
   first-class slice tasks.** Slice 5/6 pattern of catching standards gaps in the
   closing sweep is a process failure. Each implementation session applies the
   relevant `coding-standards.md` sections to its own scope (§ 2 Naming, § 5 Error
   Handling, § 6 Imports, § 7 Module Boundaries, § 8 Comments, § 12 Logging, § 13
   Async Discipline, § 14 Observability & Tracing — the gap that triggered this
   fix, § 15 Graceful Degradation). The sweep is the final gate, not the first
   inspection.

10. **`approach.md` codifies new RRP discipline as durable methodology.** Three
    additions: "What Each RRP Must Hunt For" (dead surface, dual sources of truth,
    leaky boundaries, misplaced concerns, stale residue, over-engineered surface),
    "Presenting Findings During RRP" (lead with recommendation, tight-first +
    depth-on-demand, batch small, push back when right), and "Coding-standards
    alignment is a planning concern, not a sweep concern." Future RRPs inherit.
